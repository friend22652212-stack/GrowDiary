# Grow Diary Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 16, 2026  
**Effective Date:** August 16, 2026

Grow Diary ("the App") is developed by HUA-CHIEN KAO ("we," "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App to record growth diaries for babies and pets.

---

## 1. Summary

- The App has **no** custom account system and **does not** upload your diaries or photos to our servers.
- Your data is stored **locally on your device** by default.
- If you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in **your private iCloud account** (provided by Apple).
- We **do not sell** your personal data and **do not use** third-party advertising or analytics SDKs to track you.

---

## 2. Information We Process

The App may process the following information that **you provide**:

| Data Type | Contents | Required |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Profiles | Name, type (baby/pet), birth date, notes, avatar | Optional |
| Diary entries | Title, content, date, tags | Optional |
| Photos | Images attached to entries and profile avatars | Optional |
| Growth metrics | Height, weight, head circumference, etc. | Optional |
| Milestones | Growth milestones and completion dates | Optional |

We do **not** require email, phone number, or other registration identifiers.

---

## 3. Where Data Is Stored

### 3.1 Local Storage (Default)

When iCloud is off, all diaries, photos, and growth data remain on your iPhone/iPad only.

### 3.2 iCloud Sync (Optional, Premium)

If you subscribe to Premium and enable iCloud sync, data is stored via Apple CloudKit in **your iCloud private container** (`iCloud.com.kao.growdiary`).  
This data is governed by [Apple's iCloud Terms and Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We cannot access your iCloud content.

### 3.3 Backup Files (Optional, Premium)

You may export a `.growdiary` backup file and store it in the Files app, iCloud Drive, or elsewhere. You retain full control over backup files.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

With your consent, the App may use:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Photo Library** | Select photos for diary entries |
| **Camera** | Take growth photos in-app |
| **Notifications** | Local reminders (diary, growth metrics)—not marketing |
| **Face ID / Touch ID** | App lock (Premium; biometric data handled by iOS—we cannot access it) |
| **Spotlight** | Index entries for system search (Premium; index stored locally) |

You may revoke permissions anytime in iOS Settings.

---

## 5. Subscriptions and Payments

Premium (NT$ 690/year with a 7-day free trial) is processed through the **Apple App Store**.  
We do **not** collect or store payment card details; Apple handles payment information. See [Apple Media Services Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/).

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except:

- When required by law; or
- When necessary to protect our or users' legitimate rights.

The App does **not** integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for parents or guardians to record information about **those in their care** (infants or pets).  
We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Contact us if you believe we have inadvertently received such data.

---

## 8. Retention and Deletion

- You control how long data is retained.
- Before deleting the App, consider exporting a backup (Premium).
- After uninstalling, local data is removed from the device; iCloud data must be managed via [iCloud settings](https://www.icloud.com) or Apple Support.
- On the free plan, entries older than 30 days remain on device but are locked in the App until you subscribe to Premium.

---

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data.  
Because data is primarily on your device or iCloud, you can edit or delete content in the App, or contact us for assistance.

---

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date will reflect changes. For material updates, we will notify you in the App or by other appropriate means.
